02 / Services

Furnace Cleaning

Your furnace is essential for maintaining warmth and comfort in your home, particularly during colder seasons. However, regular usage can lead to the accumulation of dirt and contaminants, potentially affecting its efficiency and safety.

Our skilled technicians conduct thorough cleaning, targeting key components like the furnace blower and heat exchangers to remove any buildup that could hinder performance. A clean furnace operates more efficiently, reducing energy costs, and distributes heat evenly throughout your home. Regular cleaning can extend your furnace's lifespan, prevent costly repairs, and mitigate fire hazards and carbon monoxide risks.

03 / Services

Dryer Vent Cleaning

Dryer vent cleaning is a crucial yet often overlooked service that not only reduces fire risks but also improves dryer efficiency. Lint and debris buildup in dryer vents can lead to hazardous conditions and longer drying times.

Kleenair's professional vent cleaning ensures unobstructed airflow, optimizing dryer performance. With specialized tools, our technicians thoroughly clean vents from the dryer to the exterior, cutting down on drying times, energy usage, and wear on the appliance.
